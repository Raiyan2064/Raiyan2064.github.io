About Me

I'm a 3rd-year Electrical & Electronic Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My core focus is Control Systems — classical control theory, state-space methods, and optimization-based control.

I'm currently working on a research paper on the robustness of PID-Integrated AGC under False Data Injection (FDI) attacks in smart grid environments — work that sits at the intersection of control theory and cybersecurity. I'm building toward graduate school in Europe (EMJMD 2027).

Before university, I was a cadet at Rangpur Cadet College — an experience that shaped how I approach discipline, teamwork, and long-term goals.

Academic Research

Research

2026 – Present  ·  RUET EEE Department

Robustness Analysis of PID-Integrated AGC Under Various False Data Injection Attacks in Smart Grid


Working under a faculty supervisor specializing in PSO-based optimization and microgrid control, toward a research publication. The work investigates how adversarial False Data Injection (FDI) attacks on smart grid measurement signals can destabilize PID-integrated Automatic Generation Control (AGC) systems — and evaluates controller robustness under various attack scenarios.


Theoretical foundation includes Particle Swarm Optimization for controller tuning, Fractional-Order PID design, Sliding Mode Control, and disturbance observer techniques. Simulation platform: MATLAB/Simulink.
