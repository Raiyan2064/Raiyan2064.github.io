About

Control theory meets cybersecurity

Hello, I am Raiyan! I am a 3rd-year Electrical & Electronic Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, with a CGPA of 3.66. My core focus is Control Systems: classical control theory, state-space methods, and optimization-based control.

I am currently working on a research paper investigating the robustness of PID-Integrated Automatic Generation Control (AGC) under False Data Injection (FDI) attacks in smart grid environments: work that sits at the intersection of control theory and cybersecurity. I am building toward graduate school in Europe (EMJMD 2027).

Before university, I was a cadet at Rangpur Cadet College: an experience that shaped how I approach discipline, teamwork, and long-term goals. Outside academics, I am curious about geopolitics, defense & aerospace, and telecommunications.

Robustness Analysis of PID-Integrated AGC Under Various False Data Injection Attacks in Smart Grid

Working under a faculty supervisor specializing in PSO-based optimization and microgrid control, toward a research publication. The work investigates how adversarial FDI attacks on smart grid measurement signals destabilize PID-integrated AGC systems, and evaluates controller robustness under various attack scenarios.

Theoretical foundation includes Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) for controller tuning, Fractional-Order PID design, Sliding Mode Control, and disturbance observer techniques. Simulation platform: MATLAB/Simulink.

IoT-Based Smart DC Microgrid

A bench-scale DC microgrid on a 6 V, 4.5 Ah lead-acid battery. An ESP32 with an INA219 sensor switches an LED, a fan and a DC motor, sheds them in priority order as the battery voltage falls, trips on overload or short circuit, and sends Telegram alerts.

Arduino-Based Ultrasonic Radar

A low-cost radar-style detector on an Arduino Uno. A servo sweeps an HC-SR04 to measure distance and angle, a second fixed HC-SR04 estimates approach speed, an RCWL-0516 senses motion, and a laptop draws the radar display.
